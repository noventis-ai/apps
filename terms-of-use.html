What Cortex does

Cortex is a personal assistant that runs a small language model on your phone. It can understand requests in chat or by voice, draft text, plan multi-step tasks and use built-in skills, such as setting alarms, opening the camera, controlling music, managing notifications and adding calendar events. It can also run automations and reminders you set up.

Features depend on your device, your Android version, the permissions you grant and whether a model has been downloaded. Some features may not be available on every phone.

On-device AI and accuracy

Cortex’s answers, drafts, plans and interpretations are generated by an AI model and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. A request like “meeting tomorrow at 5” could be read as the wrong day or time. The model has no internet access and may not know about recent events.

  • Review anything Cortex prepares before you use, send or rely on it.
  • Don’t use Cortex’s output as professional medical, legal, financial or safety advice.
  • You alone are responsible for the actions you choose to take based on what Cortex suggests, and for checking that they are correct.

On slower phones the model can take a while to respond. If it stops responding, Cortex is designed to stop waiting and tell you.

Calls, messages and actions you complete

Cortex is designed never to place a phone call or send an SMS on its own, whether you ask in chat, by voice or through an automation.

When you ask Cortex to call someone or send a text, it opens your phone’s dialer or messaging app with the number or message filled in. Nothing is dialled or sent until you press Call or Send yourself. Automations and reminders cannot call or text anyone.

Other actions that change something on your phone, such as saving a calendar event, are shown to you for confirmation first. Once you hand off to another app, what happens next is controlled by that app and its terms.

Automations and reminders

Automations run only the actions listed in the app. Cortex is designed so that if one automation fails, it shows a message and the others keep running.

Android may delay or stop background work, for example to save battery, after a restart, when the clock or time zone changes, or if you restrict the app. Because of this, Noventis does not promise that a reminder or automation will run at the exact time, or at all. Don’t rely on Cortex alone for anything where a missed or late reminder could cause harm, such as medication, travel or safety-critical tasks.
